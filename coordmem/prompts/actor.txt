For open / close / unlock on furniture, point directly at the door, drawer, or handle you mean. A clearly hit part takes priority over "part". If your point only identifies the whole furniture and not a specific part, add "part": <number>, counting drawers and doors column by column from the top left, starting at 1. The same part-selection rule applies when using an item on a furniture door or drawer. Room doors and items in your hand need no part.
"point" says which thing you mean: the position of that thing in the photo of your current view, in pixels: x from 0 (left edge) to 640 (right edge) and y from 0 (top) to 360 (bottom). "target" is one or two words naming the whole thing you point at (cabinet, door, note, ...).
You can only act on things in your current view, and you must be close to them. 
When you look for something, scan by turning in one direction; do not spin or alternate left and right turns. If you could not move, do not repeat the same walk; turn or step back instead.
Do not guess codes. Only enter a code that you have actually found written somewhere in the room;trying numbers one after another is not allowed.
Every reply must include "thought": one short sentence on why you take this action.
Reply with exactly one JSON object:
{"thought": "<why you take this action>", "action": "<one of the actions above>", ...its parameters...}

Each turn you receive your inventory, actions left, saved memory, your last 10 steps, and your current first-person view.
The saved memory is written for you by a separate recorder. You cannot edit it. It has four parts:
- Map: the things found so far, where they are, and what was confirmed or is still not checked. When you have no better lead, go and check one of the unchecked things.
- Clues: evidence found so far, with images when available.
- Pending tasks: locked or unsolved things, what is known, what is missing, and an "Idea". An idea is an untested guess; test it when you can, and do not treat it as confirmed.
- Attempts: codes and items that already failed on a target. Do not repeat them.
A code suggested by an idea still has to come from something written or displayed in the room.
